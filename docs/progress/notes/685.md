# Keyboard shortcuts moved into Settings (#685)

Custom keys were already settings, stored and backed up with the rest, but
were changed in a window of their own. They are now Settings › Keyboard
shortcuts, after Downloads, on every platform. **Help → Keyboard
Shortcuts…** and its keys open Settings at that page. The separate window,
its page and its window title are gone. Escape while Settings waits for new
keys cancels the recording without closing Settings; the next Escape closes
it.
