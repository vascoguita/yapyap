# The macOS menu bar alone (#708)

On macOS, **Start in the background** now starts YapYap at login with no
window and no Dock icon, in the menu bar alone, and **Close to the tray**
shows there as **Close to the menu bar**: closing the window also takes
YapYap off the Dock. Showing the window again, from the menu bar or by
opening YapYap, brings the Dock icon back and YapYap to the front
(`window/dock.ts`). A setting can now have a macOS label (`labelMac`), as
**Tray icon** does too (**Menu bar icon**). YapYap creates the menu bar icon on
every launch; the one missing after opening YapYap by hand was most likely
hidden by macOS, which drops icons that do not fit beside the notch and
the menus of the app in front, as the guide now says. `e2e/dock.spec.ts` checks the Dock
on macOS.
