# Spelling languages moved into Settings (#701)

The spell-check languages were settings, stored and backed up with the
rest, but were chosen only in a window of their own, reached from the
message box's right-click menu. They are now Settings › Spelling, a page
after Notifications & privacy that holds **Check spelling** (moved from
General › Writing, which is gone) and, below it, the languages: the same
search, list, limit of ten and **Use System Language**. The languages fade
while spell checking is off. The right-click menu's **Languages…**, and its
unticked **Check Spelling**, open Settings at that page. On macOS the page
says the system chooses the languages, as the window did. The separate
window, its page and its window title are gone.
