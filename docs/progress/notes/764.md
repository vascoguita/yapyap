# Reply suggestions as part of the message box (#764)

The AI assistant's suggestions no longer float above the message box as
lilac pills: the box grows upward to hold them. The tray takes the box's
exact edges, fill and shadow and is drawn just over it, and the box's top
corners go square while it sits there, so the two read as one box.
The chips take WhatsApp's neutral grey, under a quiet "Suggested replies"
heading with the sparkle and a fold arrow; a rewrite's note and Undo sit
in the same tray. While the user replies to a message, WhatsApp's quote
moves up by the tray's height: the quote first, then the suggestions,
then the box. The design (a canvas with three options, dark mode and the
states) was chosen by the maintainer: tray A, quote on top.

The message box was mapped on the maintainer's WhatsApp (structure
only): the box is the field's nearest ancestor with a fill, and the
quote sits in a footer layer of its own. The fake and showcase pages lay
their footers out the same way.
