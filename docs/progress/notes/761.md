# Senders not in the contacts, by their ~name (#761)

In a group, WhatsApp shows someone not in the user's contacts as their own
name and their number, each an `author` of its own, and the copy mark
names them by the number. The chat reader now names them as WhatsApp
shows them, `~Name`, so the AI assistant writes "~Livia" rather than
"Person 1"; someone shown only by number is still "Person 1", "Person 2".

A quote of such a sender kept the second author's text, the phone
number, inside the quoted text, so it could reach the model. Every
author is now taken out of a quote's text, in messages and in the reply
above the message box. `authoredBy` does both, beside the reader in the
page.
