# Suggestions wrap, fold away and show they are being written (#729, #709, #703)

Three changes to the suggestions row above the message box, designed
together with the design plugin and reviewed as built:

- **Wrap (#709).** The chips wrap onto more lines instead of scrolling
  sideways, each at most two lines high, so all of them show.
- **Fold (#729).** The **×** that dismissed them for good is now a chevron,
  **Hide suggestions**, which folds them into one quiet **Suggestions**
  chip. They stay folded, in every chat, until the user shows them again.
  They keep being made meanwhile, so showing them needs no wait. The
  `dismiss` action is replaced by `collapse` and `expand`.
- **Writing (#703).** While the model writes suggestions, three blank chips
  shimmer in their place (still under reduced motion), and screen readers
  hear "Writing suggestions…".
