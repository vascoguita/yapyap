# Auto picks the best model for writing (#702)

Auto, and the model Settings recommends, used each provider's quick tier:
Claude's Haiku and OpenAI's mini models. They wrote noticeably worse
replies and rewrites than the provider's best general model. Auto now
picks Claude's newest Sonnet, and OpenAI's newest flagship, named by its
number alone, such as gpt-5.5, without mini, nano or pro. Opus and pro
models stay out, as they cost and take more than a short message needs.
On a model server, Auto keeps the smallest model Ollama reports, which
answers in time on home hardware. The Model hint and the guide say so.
