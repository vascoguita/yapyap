# Local E2E runs keep the desktop's document portal (#713)

The documented E2E and screenshot commands give their private session bus a
throwaway `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, with the desktop's keyring linked into it. Its document portal used to mount on the
desktop's `/run/user/<uid>/doc` and unmount it on exit, which stopped Flatpak
apps such as a Flatpak browser from starting.
