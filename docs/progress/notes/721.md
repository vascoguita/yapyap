# Reply suggestions no longer stop for the session (#721)

On 0.3.1-beta.7, reply suggestions stopped for a whole session while
rewrites kept working, until a restart. The assistant follows the open
chat with a look every second and a half, one at a time, and a look the
WhatsApp page never answered held up every look after it. Now a look that
has not answered after four turns, about six seconds, is given up: the next
one starts, and the late answer, if it ever comes, is ignored. WhatsApp's
page still matched every selector the look uses.
