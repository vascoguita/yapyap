# Get started

Six short steps from installing YapYap to using it every day. Each links to
the full guide when you want the details.

## 1. Install YapYap

Download YapYap for Linux, Windows or macOS from the
[download page](/guide/install). On Linux, the
[package repositories](/guide/install#linux-repositories) keep it up to date
with the rest of your system.

## 2. Link your phone

Open YapYap. It shows WhatsApp's QR code, as WhatsApp Web does. On your
phone, open **WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device** and scan it. Your
chats appear in a few seconds; your phone does not need to stay online.

The first time, a short welcome asks for a few choices, such as the theme
and whether closing the window keeps YapYap in the tray. You can change all
of them later in [Settings](/guide/#settings).

![The main window, with the Personal and Work accounts in the sidebar](/screenshots/main.webp)

## 3. Add a second account

Choose **Account → Add Account** and link the other phone the same way.
With two accounts or more, a sidebar lists them with their unread chats; click
one to switch. **Account → Manage Accounts…** renames them and gives each a
color. [More about accounts](/guide/#accounts).

![The Accounts window, where each account has a name and a color](/screenshots/accounts.webp)

## 4. Notifications and the tray

New messages show as your system's notifications, from every account.
Clicking one opens the chat. The tray icon shows that something is unread;
its menu has **Do Not Disturb**, and Settings → General can keep YapYap
running in the tray when you close the window.
[More about notifications](/guide/#notifications).

## 5. Write with the AI assistant

The AI assistant is built in. Right above the message box, it suggests
replies, writes the message you describe, rewrites your draft and catches
you up on unread chats, for you to check and send. Set it up in
**Settings → Assistant**: a private model that runs on your computer, a
model server you already run, or your own key for Claude or OpenAI.
[Set up the assistant](/guide/ai-assistant).

![Settings for the AI assistant](/screenshots/assistant.webp)

## 6. Add extensions

Extensions add features to YapYap. Open **File → Extensions…** (on macOS,
**YapYap → Extensions…**), pick one in the
**Marketplace** tab, and choose **Install**. YapYap shows what it asks to do,
and installs nothing until you agree. Extensions marked **Third-party, not
reviewed** come from their authors' own servers and are installed at your
own risk. You can take any permission back
later. [Browse the marketplace](/marketplace/).

![The Marketplace tab, listing the call recorder and a third-party extension](/screenshots/extensions.webp)
